Description

Immerse yourself in the culinary opulence of Hyderabad with this Shahi Hyderabadi Paneer Biryani, a majestic vegetarian masterpiece that rivals any ROYAL FEAST. Every forkful offers a symphony of flavors, where long, fragrant grains of premium Basmati rice are perfectly layered with melt-in-your-mouth paneer cubes marinated in a rich, velvety spiced yogurt blend. Infused with the regal essence of saffron milk, ghee, and sweet caramelized onions (birista), this slow-cooked dum biryani delivers an unparalleled smoky aroma and depth of flavor

To elevate this royal affair, it is paired alongside a cool, refreshing onion-mint raita. The creamy, whisked yogurt base laced with roasted cumin, crisp onions, and fresh herbs acts as the ultimate cooling companion, cutting through the intense heat of the complex Hyderabadi spices. Served together in traditional earthenware, this pairing is not just a meal, but a celebrate-worthy celebration of authentic Indian heritage right on your plate

Ingredients for making this Royal Feast

  • For Rice: 2 cups Long-grain Basmati Rice (soaked for 30 mins), Whole spices (Bay leaf, Cardamom, Cloves, Cinnamon, Shahi Jeera), Salt, and 1 tsp Ghee.
  • For Paneer Marination: 300g Paneer cubes, 1 cup Thick curd, 1 tbsp Ginger-garlic paste, 1 tbsp Biryani Masala, Red chili powder, Turmeric, Lemon juice, and Salt.
  • For Layering & Dum: ½ cup Fried onions (Birista), ¼ cup Fresh Mint & Coriander leaves, 2 tbsp Saffron milk, and 2 tbsp Melted Ghee.
  • For Raita: 1 cup Fresh Curd, ½ cup Finely chopped onions, 1 tbsp Chopped mint, Roasted cumin powder, and Black salt to taste.

STEPS

  1. Parboil the Rice: Boil water with whole spices, salt, and ghee. Add soaked Basmati rice and cook until it is 80% done, then drain completely and set aside.
  2. Marinate the Paneer: Whisk curd with ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, chili powder, biryani masala, mint, coriander, and salt. Coat the paneer cubes evenly and marinate for 20–30 minutes.
  3. Cook the Masala Base: Heat oil and ghee in a heavy-bottomed pot. Sauté the marinated paneer gently with a handful of fried onions for 4–5 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Layer the Biryani: Reduce the heat. Evenly spread the parboiled rice over the paneer masala layer. Top with remaining fried onions, fresh mint, coriander, and drizzle the saffron milk and ghee over the surface.
  5. Dum Cooking: Seal the pot tightly with an airtight lid or aluminum foil. Cook on low heat (ideally on top of a heavy tawa) for 15 minutes to trap the steam and let flavors fuse.
  6. Prepare Raita & Serve: Whisk the fresh curd smoothly, fold in the chopped onions, mint, cumin powder, and salt. Fluff the hot biryani gently and serve fresh alongside the chilled raita.
